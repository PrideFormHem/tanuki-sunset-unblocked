## 🧠 Under The Hood: How Unblockers Work

When you try to access a gaming site, your network traffic is inspected by the school or office firewall. If the URL is on a blocklist, the request is denied.

Unblockers work by using a **proxy server**. Instead of connecting directly to the game website, your browser connects to the proxy. The proxy fetches the game for you and sends the data back to your browser.

The key is that the firewall only sees your connection to the proxy, not the game itself. Our repository curates the best, most reliable proxies that are fast enough to stream the game without lag.
